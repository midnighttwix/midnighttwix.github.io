Go Farfetch

One Giver. One Guesser. Run around the house and fetch real objects that describe your Pokémon — no words, no gestures, just stuff.

How it works

  1. The Giver looks at the screen alone and sees a Pokémon.
  2. They hide the screen, then go fetch objects from around the house that hint at it.
  3. The Guesser sees only the objects, then types their guess here.
  4. Get 3 right to win. A miss costs you 10 seconds.
